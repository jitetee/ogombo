Privacy.

Short version: only what's needed to run the site, nothing sold.

What is stored

  • Comments, reactions and shares — the name you type and your comment are saved and shown publicly. Don't post personal details.
  • Contact form — name, email/phone and message are saved so I can reply.
  • Orders for paid materials — name, phone, email, delivery address (WhatsApp/SMS/email) and the M-Pesa message you paste are saved to verify payment and send your file. Only the site admin can read them.
  • Newsletter — your email, only to send updates. Ask and it will be removed.
  • Security record — when you post a comment, send a message, make a request or place an order, your IP address, browser type, time zone, the page and the time are saved separately (your IP address is looked up through a free IP-lookup service at that moment). It is not shown publicly or in the admin screens. It is kept to stop abuse and to answer a lawful request (a court order or a police report), and is deleted after 12 months unless it is needed for an open case.
  • Analytics — anonymous page-view counts (page, day, device type, referring site). No cookies, no tracking across sites; Do-Not-Track is respected.

On your device

Your browser remembers your theme, the name you used to comment and which posts you reacted to (so a like can be undone). Clearing site data removes this.

Third parties

Files are delivered through Google Drive, the database runs on Google Firebase, and PDFs are generated in your browser. Share buttons open the network you choose.

Your rights (Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019)

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data held about you, and to object to its use. This site does not record keystrokes, does not sell data and does not track you across other sites.

Your requests

To remove a comment, message, order or subscription, email me.
