WHOAMI

24, Kenyan, quiet until provoked by a good idea.

Born August 2nd. Grew up around Matunda, Moi's Bridge. Two things run my life right now: theatre technology, and making that world easier to understand for other people.

education

Diploma in Perioperative Theatre Technology (2023–2025, graduated December 2025; institution listed on my CV). Practical score 78%.

Field attachment — Likuyani Sub-County Hospital, Feb–Apr 2024. Scrubbed for caesarean section cases, ran sterilisation (CSSD), theatre setup and turnover, WHO Surgical Safety Checklist adherence.

Certifications — Quality Improvement (Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust), Basic Life Support, Infection Prevention & Control.

day-job

Alongside creating content and looking for theatre practice, I work the front desk at a cyber café — JULO Enterprises. Printing, typing, eCitizen and KRA services, and teaching basic computer skills to walk-in clients. Temporary, but it's kept my tech instincts sharp.

The content side

I turn what I'm learning into short, memorable posts — anatomy mnemonics, OT quizzes, positioning and safety checklists — for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook. If a concept took me three readings to actually get, there's a good chance it'll take you thirty seconds once I've turned it into a graphic. See the content page.

The theatre side

Perioperative work is precise and mostly invisible — nobody notices the person who made sure the theatre ran smoothly, and that's exactly the appeal. No room for guessing. Every step has a reason.

Also true about me

Introvert. Talks more once you land on the right topic — usually anatomy, sometimes Kenyan history and culture (I'm slowly writing books on both — see the writing page). Coding is a hobby on the side, mostly playing with AI tools for fun — ask me on WhatsApp if you're curious, but it's not the main thing I do.
